## Why it is still worth five minutes

**Because on substance it is one of the closest job descriptions of this entire search**, and
that is useful information even though she cannot have it.

| Their line | Her job |
| --- | --- |
| Source new Healthcare deals and develop them into quality transactions | 30 to 50 proposals a month screened, deep-dive diligence on the survivors |
| Build a pipeline of companies investing in **early-stage, high-impact projects** | 40+ programmes originated across health technology, AI diagnostics, maternal, immunisation, geriatric, tribal and primary care |
| **Defend investment recommendations before approval bodies** | Funding recommendations to the CEO and the **Board of Trustees, the body approving every commitment** |
| Risk assessment covering financial, **ESG** and onboarding risk | Diligence, founder and programme leader assessment, heavier evidence bars on technology pilots |
| Overall responsibility for **portfolio quality, performance and monitoring** | Portfolio dashboards to the CEO, utilisation tracking, variance analysis, unit cost against plan |
| Monitor deals after contracting | Full lifecycle from origination through execution, monitoring and closure |

**That is development finance**, and development finance is the single best-matched category
her profile has found: public or quasi-public capital, impact mandate, origination rather
than deal execution, healthcare specialism treated as an asset. The work is right. The
passport and the language are not.

### What to take from it, which is a search term rather than an application

**"Business Developer" is the title development finance uses for origination.** It has not
appeared in any of the five scans run so far, which have used strategy, investment,
portfolio, partnerships and founder's office as the search vocabulary. Worth adding, along
with **business development officer** and **investment officer**, both of which are the
standard DFI titles for this work.

**Where to point it.** India-facing development finance institutions and their local offices:
the obvious names are IFC, British International Investment, Proparco, FMO (the Dutch
development bank, which does have India exposure), KfW / DEG, JICA, and the Indian
development finance and blended finance outfits. **Farro Capital's Asia Blended Fund, row
064 at FIT 4.6, is already the highest-FIT role of the search and sits in exactly this
category.** That is not a coincidence, and it is the strongest argument yet for treating
blended and development finance as the primary lane rather than one of several.

**The caution, stated honestly.** ADB was already lost on financial modelling, and most DFI
origination roles ask for executed transactions she does not have. Farro is the exception
that scoped modelling away explicitly. So the lane is right and the entry point is narrow:
**the roles to chase are the ones where origination and appraisal are the job and execution
sits with someone else.**
