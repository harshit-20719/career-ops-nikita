## The line that settles it

> **"0–1 year of experience; freshers welcome"**

**She has four years and three months, a ₹450 crore portfolio, and funding recommendations
taken to a Board of Trustees.** This req is written for someone with no track record at all.

This is the mirror image of row 060, Global HealthX, which was rejected for asking 8 to 12
years. **It is the same gate, pointed the other way, and it is just as real.** The search has
spent two months establishing that stated bands get applied at the screening stage before
anyone reads the substance. A band works in both directions: an application from someone at
4x the stated experience gets filtered as a mismatch, or gets read as not having understood
the posting.

**And the problem is not the pay.** She has been clear that money is not the axis of this
search, so a fresher-band salary is not in itself a reason to decline. The problem is scope.
**"No comp floor" is not the same as "no scope floor."** In a seat designed for a fresher,
her four years buy her nothing: not more responsibility, not a better title, not a faster
path. She would be the most experienced person in the room doing the least senior work, and
in two years' time it reads as a step backwards on the CV rather than sideways.

## The one thing that would change the answer

**Which company it is.** Not to rescue this req, but because the right move might be a
different one:

- **If it is a company she actively wants** and it is Bangalore based and growing, then the
  move is **not this posting**. It is a direct note to the founder, which is how every open
  door in this search has been found. A company posting a founder's office at 0 to 1 years is
  a company that knows it needs leverage and has budgeted for the cheapest version of it.
  Whether it would pay for the senior version is a question only the founder can answer, and
  applying to the fresher req forecloses it. **Once she is in the system at that band, the
  band is what she is.**
- **If it is a company she is lukewarm about**, this is an easy no and costs nothing.

**Founder's office titles are also notoriously mislabelled**, and the band may be HR written
rather than founder written. That is a real possibility and it is worth exactly one message
to test, not an application. The test is simple: does the founder treat "0 to 1 year" as a
filter or as a description of the last person they hired?
