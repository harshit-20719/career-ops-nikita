## 1. Healthify — Founder's Office, Strategy · Bangalore · FIT 3.7 / ODDS 3.7

LinkedIn `4470904210`, posted ~1 week ago, 200+ applicants.

**The title is misleading in her favour. This is not a founder's office job — it is a
pharma partnerships leadership role**, and partnerships is the strongest thing on her CV
after the heat map.

Healthify is building **India's leading AI-powered GLP-1 and obesity management platform.**
The role owns "strategic partnerships with leading pharmaceutical companies in obesity,
diabetes, and metabolic health" — commercial discussions, contract negotiation, alliance
management, and designing **Patient Support Programs** for GLP-1 therapies.

**Why this one is the pick of the scan:**

- **"2+ years of experience"** in strategy consulting, healthcare, pharma, digital health,
  business development or partnerships. **That is the widest band she has seen all search**,
  and she is well clear of it.
- **"MBA or equivalent postgraduate degree from a leading institution preferred."**
  *Preferred*, not required — and she holds a **P.G.D. from Ashoka University**, which is a
  postgraduate degree from a leading institution. **After nineteen roles killed on a degree
  line, this one she arguably satisfies outright.**
- **Partnerships is the core of the job**, and she has originated and closed 15+
  institutional partnerships from scratch, structuring terms.
- **The market-access work already done is reusable.** Patient Support Programs are the
  commercial arm of market access, and she has the Novartis (row 041) and Pfizer (row 042)
  research behind her — both of which she lost on degree lines, not on substance.
- **There is a named route in:** the job poster is **Prerna T., Strategic Talent
  Acquisition and HR leader**, and LinkedIn offers "direct message the job poster."

**FIT 3.7** — background 4, scope 4 (owns commercial relationships with pharma companies),
alumni and company signal **3 `no-data`**, transition value 4, trajectory 4, flexibility 3.

**ODDS 3.7** — profile match 4, referral path 3 (named recruiter, DM-able), speed 4.

**What she does not have:** pharma-side partnership experience specifically, and
contract-negotiation depth. The JD asks for "strong understanding of the pharmaceutical
ecosystem, patient support programs, obesity management, diabetes care" as *preferred*,
which is the right side of the line.

**Action: apply, and DM Prerna the same day.** Per the rubric this is "apply this week,
worth proper tailoring."

## 2. Dozee — Founders Office, Program Manager · Bengaluru · FIT 3.9 / ODDS 3.3

LinkedIn `4475582190`, posted **the same day as this scan**.

**Highest FIT of the scan**, and the closest thing to a true founder's-office seat in
healthcare that this search has found in Bengaluru.

Dozee is **India's No. 1 contactless Remote Patient Monitoring company** — AI-powered RPM
and early-warning systems, **300+ hospitals, 16,000+ beds across four countries, 1M+
patients monitored, 35,000+ life-saving alerts**, and the world's first non-contact blood
pressure monitoring system. Covered by Bloomberg.

The JD is unusually clear about what it is not: *"This is not a traditional Program
Manager, Executive Assistant, or strategy-only role. It is a high-trust, high-ownership
role for someone structured, sharp, and execution-oriented."* It sits at the intersection
of program management, business operations, executive stakeholder management and founder's
office.

**Why it scores highest on fit:**

- **The work is what she already does.** Translate C-suite priorities into structured
  plans with owners and timelines; own cross-functional programs and business reviews;
  run the leadership cadence; synthesize data into leadership dashboards; represent the
  CxO's office externally. She presents **investment briefs and portfolio dashboards
  directly to the CEO**, reports to the Board of Trustees, and carries two mandates across
  40+ programmes simultaneously.
- **3–5 years. She is at 4y2m — dead centre.**
- **Education: "BTech/MBA or any equivalent qualification."** That "or any equivalent" is a
  soft gate, not a wall, and it is the difference between this and the nineteen that died.
- Healthcare, and specifically **health AI in hospitals** — her health-tech and AI
  diagnostics sub-themes.
- Bengaluru, P0.

**FIT 3.9** — background 4, scope 4, signal 4, transition value 4, trajectory 4,
flexibility 3 (Bengaluru onsite, relocation from Mumbai).

**ODDS 3.3** — profile match 4, referral path **2** (none found), speed 4.

**The gap she must address:** *"Exposure to AI products or AI-led workflow automation, with
the ability to engage credibly on product and technical topics,"* and *"leveraging AI, LLMs,
and automation to improve tracking, reporting, analysis."* Her AI exposure is as a funder
evaluating AI diagnostics, not as someone using AI tooling in their own workflow. **That is
answerable** — unlike Eka Care's product roles, this asks for credible engagement, not
shipped product.

**One oddity:** the posting says two hours old and over 200 applicants, which does not add
up. Probably a repost, so the clock may be older than it looks. Apply quickly either way.

**ODDS is held down entirely by the referral path**, which the rubric flags as the one
dimension she can change. A warm introduction here moves ODDS from 3.3 to about 4.4.
