## 1 · Makers Hive — the find of the scan

`linkedin.com/jobs/view/4473641089` · Hyderabad · posted 4 days ago · 200+ applicants ·
tagged Executive

**They invented KalArm, India's first fully functional bionic hand**, designed and
manufactured in India. The portfolio spans **bionic prosthetics, assistive devices and
dexterous robotics** across Indian and international markets, and **they are preparing their
Series A.**

The role works directly with the CEO across four things: **the full capital raising cycle,
investor relations and board operations, corporate strategy, and how the company is
positioned to investors and the market.** Their own words for what it leads to: "direct CEO,
board and investor exposure; a clear path to a strategy or business-line leadership role."

### Why this matters more than it looks

**She asked yesterday for a way to learn how commercial capital makes decisions, and was
willing to do it unpaid.** This is a paid seat **on the receiving side of exactly that
process.** Data room, diligence responses, investor targeting by thesis and cheque size,
term sheets, board packs, a Series A from first contact to close.

**That is arguably a better education than a twelve week fellowship inside a fund**, because
the person running the company's side of a raise sees every question an investor asks and
every weakness those questions expose. It also pays, it is in a P0 city, and it is in medical
devices.

### What maps, and it is a lot

| Their requirement | Her record |
| --- | --- |
| **Board operations**: board packs, pre-reads, minutes, resolutions, tracking commitments to closure | Four years preparing briefs and dashboards for the CEO and the **Board of Trustees**, the body approving every commitment |
| **Investor outreach and pipeline**: research the target list, qualify funds by thesis, stage, cheque size and portfolio fit, map warm introduction pathways | **Pitched Tiff.in to around twenty funds.** And this is literally the work in her own repo this month |
| **Due diligence management**: single point of coordination, log and route every question, maintain the tracker | Diligence on 30 to 50 proposals a month, from the funder's side of the same table |
| **Corporate strategy and reporting**: consolidate revenue, pipeline and margin actuals against plan and flag variances with causes | **Variance analysis and utilisation tracking against plan**, unit cost modelling, portfolio dashboards |
| **Writing**: "investor-grade writing is a core requirement of this role and will be assessed" | Published paper in the *Journal of Enterprising Culture*, policy briefs for senior management, board papers |
| Senior stakeholders with composure, high discretion | CEO and Board of Trustees, and field work across the remotest districts in India |

**And the qualification line is the open-door pattern again:** *"MBA, Chartered Accountant
**or equivalent qualification**; candidates from management consulting, transaction and deal
advisory, financial research, investment banking, venture capital, **or an early operating
role at a funded company** are all relevant."* Both halves let her in. **Twenty one roles
this search have died on a credential line; this one is written the other way.**

### The gap, stated plainly

> **"Financial modelling: the ability to build a driver-based operating model independently
> and to defend every assumption within it."**

And one of the five strategic priorities is *"rebuild the operating model from first
principles… from activity drivers rather than from targets, with every assumption documented
and defensible under diligence."*

**This is the heaviest version of the financial modelling requirement she has met.** It is
not a passing mention in a bullet list; it is a named deliverable. Financial modelling has
now blocked **eleven lanes**: ADB, Artha, Navi, Millennium, Pfizer, Rock Health, Pomoro, 2070
Portfolio Associate, Beyond, 24 Ventures below, and this one.

**But the distinction that mattered for 2070 Health still holds, and it cuts her way here.**
This is a **skill line, not a credential line.** And a driver-based operating model is the
kind she is closest to: budget allocation from activity drivers, utilisation against plan,
cost per unit, and the Tiff.in work where she built market sizing and unit economics into
investor-ready projections. **What she does not have is valuation.** This role does not ask
for valuation. It asks for an operating model, which is the half she has touched.

**She should not pretend otherwise, and the application has a natural place to say so.**

### How to apply, and why the process is good news

> **Send your CV and a short note under 300 words to `hr@makershive.io`. Tell us one thing
> you noticed about Makers Hive and what you would do about it.** Then a short assignment,
> then walk the CEO through it. Subject line: "Associate, Founder's Office - Strategy & IR".
> A link to something you have built or written is welcome but optional.

**No portal, a named inbox, a written prompt, and the founder at the end.** That is the
process shape every open door in this search has had. The 300 word prompt is also the single
best thing on this scan for her: *one thing you noticed and what you would do about it* is
exactly the four-questions structure that the Qurist write up used, and she has an afternoon's
research advantage because assistive-device reimbursement and procurement in India is adjacent
to what she funded.

**And "a link to something you have written" is an open invitation** for the sector landscape
recommended yesterday for the venture outreach. One document now serves both.

**Action: apply this week.** Scored properly it lands around **FIT 4.0 / ODDS 3.4** — the
operating-model requirement holds ODDS down, the 200+ applicant count holds it down further,
and everything else is in her favour.

## 2 · 24 Ventures — the first posted venture role that does not gate her

`linkedin.com/jobs/view/4476259535` · Lavelle Road, Bengaluru · posted 1 day ago · 200
applicants · work from office · **3 to 10 years**

A sector-agnostic investment platform. The role is **deal sourcing and investment
origination**: identify opportunities against the thesis, build relationships across founders,
accelerators, incubators, bankers and advisors, run preliminary evaluation of business models
and markets, **prepare investment teasers, screening notes and opportunity summaries**, track
sector and funding trends, maintain the pipeline and CRM, and **support the investment team in
preparing Investment Committee materials.**

**Strip the vocabulary and that is her job description for the last four years.** Screening
notes, preliminary evaluation, opportunity summaries, committee materials, pipeline. The
Trusts called it proposals and the Board of Trustees; a fund calls it deals and the IC.

**Why this is notable.** On 2026-09-07 this search established that **healthcare VC in India
produced zero postings across seven funds**, and that the venture lane was outreach-only.
Every deal-side role since has come from her feed or a link she sent. **This is the first
posted origination role in one of her cities to surface in a keyword scan**, and it came up
under the exact terms yesterday's Invest International eval recommended adding. **That
recommendation paid off in one day.**

### Two real problems

**Valuation.** *"Conduct preliminary financial analysis and valuation assessment"* and
*"understanding of valuation methodologies and investment fundamentals."* She has diligence
and performance analysis; she does not have DCF, comparables or cap tables. **Unlike Makers
Hive, this one does ask for valuation specifically.**

**The preferred background list.** AIF, venture capital, private equity, investment banking,
corporate venture capital, investment advisory or transaction advisory. **She has none of the
six.** That is a soft screen, not a gate, but with 200 applicants in a day it will be used.

**What is in her favour:** *"MBA – Finance **preferred**. CA / CFA Level I or II **will be an
advantage**."* Preferred and advantage, not required. Three to ten years covers her 4y3m. And
the role is explicitly scoped as supporting the investment team through **the early stages of
the deal lifecycle**, which is the origination and appraisal end rather than execution,
exactly the narrow entry point the Invest International eval identified.

**Action: apply, and do not tailor the CV around finance she does not have.** Lead with
sourcing, screening, diligence and the Board-level recommendation record, and let the
valuation line be a question they ask rather than a claim she makes. Around **FIT 3.6 / ODDS
2.6**.

## 3 · Unbox Health — the one with 31 applicants instead of 200

`linkedin.com/jobs/view/4476273047` · Bengaluru, hybrid · posted 1 day ago · **31
applicants** · 2+ years

**India's first ratings platform for packaged foods and health supplements.** They
independently lab test products and publish unbiased ratings. It is an **open application**
rather than a defined role, and the problems they list are brand, distribution, revenue,
partnerships, operations, *"or come define your own problems."*

**The line that makes this worth her time:**

> **"You go to the evidence first, and you change your mind when the data says so."**

That is a fair description of four years of setting evidence bars and deciding which health
technologies genuinely worked versus which had worked once in favourable conditions. **A
company whose entire product is independent testing of health claims is in the evidence
business**, which is the rarest thing on her board: a consumer company where her actual
discipline is the product rather than a transferable skill.

**No degree gate. No credential line. Titles explicitly discounted** — *"we care more about
what you've built than the titles you've held."* And 31 applicants against 200 everywhere
else on this scan.

**Two honest caveats.** The register is consumer and FMCG, and they want someone for whom
*"health, wellness and nutrition aren't just a category, they're personal"* — someone who
flips the protein bar over to read the label. **If that is not true of her, this is the wrong
application**, because they will ask and it will show. And it spends the healthcare
differentiator partially, though less than FirstClub or Fireside did, since the subject
matter is at least health.

**Action: worth the form, which is the only accepted route** (`forms.gle/kjEEP5iVs2fb9rtM7`).
Low effort, low competition, and she can answer the "define your own problem" prompt with
something real: how a ratings platform should handle supplement claims where the evidence base
is thin, which is the exact judgement she has been making. Around **FIT 3.2 / ODDS 3.6**.

Worth noting for the record: this is the **second** Peak XV posting to explicitly forbid
contacting their people, after row 066. That is now a standing feature of this firm rather
than one posting's wording.

## What this scan says about the search

**1. Hyderabad finally produced something, and it is the best role on the board this week.**
Three previous scans returned almost nothing there that cleared her gates, and the
recommendation had been to treat Bengaluru as the second city and Hyderabad as opportunistic.
**Makers Hive is the argument for keeping Hyderabad in the rotation.**

**2. The "or equivalent" test held for the fifth time.** Makers Hive says "MBA, Chartered
Accountant or equivalent" and lists an early operating role at a funded company as a relevant
route. 24 Ventures says "MBA Finance preferred". NeoDocs names a degree field and is
therefore out. **The test is now reliable enough to use as a first filter on any posting
before reading the rest of it.**

**3. Yesterday's search-term change worked immediately.** The Invest International eval
recommended adding origination vocabulary to the scan. **"Venture capital" in Bengaluru and
"investment officer" India-wide both returned 24 Ventures**, a posted origination role in a
lane that had produced zero postings in seven funds a month ago. One day, one result. The
terms stay in the rotation.

**4. "Chief of staff" in Bengaluru returned literally zero listings in seven days**, and
"founder's office" in Bengaluru returned mostly executive assistant and PMO roles. The
founder's office title is being used for EA work in that market this week. **Hyderabad's
version of the same search returned the two best results of the scan.** That inversion is
worth watching rather than treating as noise.

**5. The modelling finding has sharpened into two findings.** Eleven lanes have now asked for
financial modelling, but **valuation and operating models are not the same ask.** She has
touched operating models through budgets, unit cost and the Tiff.in projections. She has not
touched valuation at all. **Postings that want an operating model are worth applying to.
Postings that want valuation methodologies are a longer shot.** Makers Hive is the first and
24 Ventures is the second, which is why they score differently on odds despite both being
good fits on substance.
