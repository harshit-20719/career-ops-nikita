scan.

## What this actually means

This is not bad luck across twelve applications. It is one market telling her the same
thing twelve times.

**Indian pharma and life-sciences corporate strategy is credential-gated at entry.** The
gate is an MBBS, a B.Pharm, a life-sciences PhD, or an MBA from a named institution — and
it is applied at the screening stage, before anyone reads what she has done. Her actual
record is strong enough that she keeps clearing the substance bar and losing at the
paperwork. That will not change by writing better applications.

**Three of these were caught only because the full JD was read.** Glenmark's Senior
Manager role looked like a clean second chance after the first Glenmark posting failed;
its requirement is *harder* than the first. ClearView had been reopened this morning on
the grounds that its posting was live again — the real disqualifier was a life-sciences
degree all along, and the row is now closed on merit rather than availability.

## The doors that are actually open

Every role that has scored well and stayed open shares one feature: **no institutional
credential in the requirements**, usually because a founder or a small team wrote the
posting rather than an HR function screening at volume.

| What works | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| **Founder's offices and startups** | HospiPal (no degree gate, healthcare not required), Clinikally (2+ yrs, any background), Cube (3+ yrs), Voy (4–8 yrs, no degree), Honasa (Bachelor's in Economics explicitly listed, MBA optional) |
| **Postings with no stated bar at all** | Piramal Pharma Solutions — no degree, no years, Mid-Senior tag |
| **Roles where a human reads the application** | Good Health (named recruiter), HospiPal ("do not send just a CV") |
| **Cold outreach, where there is no screen to fail** | Cent, Kae Capital, Evervie, the Bansal foundation |

## The recommendation

**Stop applying to large pharma, medtech and life-sciences consulting strategy roles.**
They are the most obvious fit on paper and they have a 0-for-8 record, all on the same
line. Each one costs an hour of tailoring and returns a filter.

Redirect that hour to the four categories above. Three of the five strongest positions on
the board right now — HospiPal 3.7/4.0, Piramal 4.2/2.7, Bansal 4.0/4.0 — come from
postings that set no credential bar, and the two cold-outreach targets have no screen at
all.

**The one exception worth keeping** is IQVIA (4.2/2.0), where the tier-1 institution
requirement is marked *preferred* rather than required. That is the only large-firm
posting on the board that leaves the door open, and it is also the one where a referral
matters most.

### The recommendation has not changed, and the record has got worse

0-for-8 became **0-for-14 on large pharma, medtech and life-sciences strategy.** Every
one was a genuine fit on substance. Cipla's accountabilities are close enough to her real
work to be irritating: portfolio collation across geographies, prioritisation using
revenue potential and lifecycle stage, variance analysis against launch KPIs, and
"insight-driven portfolio presentations for senior leadership." She does all of that on a
$51M portfolio. It does not matter, because the degree line is applied before a human
reads any of it.

**Large-pharma corporate strategy should now be treated as closed, not as a lane with bad
odds.** A lane that has returned nothing in fourteen attempts on an unchangeable
criterion is not a lane.

### What replaced it, and why that is the better use of the hour

The doors listed above have held up. Since 2026-09-07 the postings that scored highest
all had no credential bar: ACT Fellowship (no MBA, no CA, no B.Pharm, no tier-1), Rock
Health Advisory ("Bachelor's degree", emphasis only *desirable*), Weekday AI (2–10 years,
no degree requirement anywhere), Darwinbox Chief of Staff (no years bar, no degree gate),
and **2070 Health New Ventures — 4.3/4.0, the best combined score of the search, whose
stated bar names healthcare as a qualifying route in its own right.**

That is the pattern, stated as plainly as it can be: **the roles she can actually get are
the ones where someone writes the posting to describe a job, rather than to filter a
queue.**
