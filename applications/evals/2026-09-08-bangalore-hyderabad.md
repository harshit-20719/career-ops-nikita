## What is genuinely open to her there

All three are chief of staff at technology companies. None is healthcare.

### 1. Incresco — Chief of Staff to the CEO · Bengaluru · **the best of the three**

Three business lines: a technology consulting practice and two product businesses. The
mandate has real teeth: *"Department heads report to the CEO on their function, and to you
on their numbers."*

**Its requirement list reads closer to her actual record than anything found in this
search except Bridgespan:**

- *4–8 years running operations, a function, or a business unit with its own outcomes* —
  she is at 4y2m, at the floor but inside
- *"Direct ownership of a number you were personally answerable for"* — cost-per-beneficiary
  and the 60% proactive-target improvement
- *"Proven ability to hold people to commitments across teams you do not manage"* — **this
  is her financial-gatekeeper role exactly.** She reviewed every partner budget in the
  health theme, not only her own programmes. It is her most distinctive and least-used
  credential, and here it is written into the requirements
- Preferred list, where she hits four of five: built management reporting where none
  existed, ran a recurring operating review attended by function heads, worked directly
  with a founder or CEO, started something from zero

**The risk:** *"MBA or equivalent commercial training."* A PGD in Liberal Studies is not
commercial training. Softer than "MBA required," but real. 200+ applicants. Bangalore
onsite.

**Scored: FIT 3.3 / ODDS 3.0.**

### 2. Weekday AI — Chief of Staff, Office of the CEO · Hyderabad

**Experience band is 2–10 years**, which is the widest found anywhere, and no degree
requirement appears. Reports to the Co-Founder and CEO, spanning Product, Engineering,
AI/ML, Partnerships, Operations and Institutional Engagements. Hyderabad, work from office.
YC W21.

### 3. Darwinbox — Chief of Staff · Hyderabad

A genuine Indian SaaS unicorn, reporting to a Co-Founder. No years bar and no degree gate
stated. Requires *"hands-on AI/automation fluency"*, which is unestablished in her
materials and would be tested.

## The trade this forces

Her best odds in Bangalore and Hyderabad come from **spending her healthcare
differentiator**, because the healthcare employers in both cities screen on credentials
she does not have and the technology companies do not.

That is the same decision already sitting unresolved on rows `020-cube` and `027-honasa`.
It is now the third time the search has produced it, which is a signal that it should be
answered deliberately rather than deferred again.

## Honest recommendation

**No kit built.** Incresco at 3.3 / 3.0 is solid, not outstanding, and the rubric's own
rule for that band is *"only if the pipeline is thin."* Hers is not thin: eight finished
kits are unsent. Building a ninth would be adding to the wrong end of the problem.

If she decides the healthcare-versus-chief-of-staff question in favour of the latter,
Incresco is where to start and the kit takes an hour.
