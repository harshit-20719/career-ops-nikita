**What she clears:** healthcare domain, Bengaluru, **"2+ years of experience"** so she is

**What the job actually is, and the problem:**

> "**Design and stand up the offline sales function from scratch**… **Hire, train, and
> manage the on-ground sales team** as it's built out… Visit distributors, channel partners,
> customers PAN India"

That is an offline sales build with a founder's-office label on it. The requirement says
"ideally in a startup, **operating roles, growth, sales, or general management**" — and she
has none of those four. **She has never sold anything and has never managed a team.** Those
are not peripheral to this role; they are the first two bullets.

**And the referral channel is explicitly closed**, which is the second posting in two days
to do that: *"Please apply to this role directly on LinkedIn. This is the only way to be
considered for this role. **Please do not DM any of our stakeholders about this
position.**"* There is a named poster, Suraj Laungani, VP Human Capital at Peak XV, and
messaging him is forbidden by instruction.

FIT: background **2**, scope 4, signal **5** (a Peak XV portfolio founder's-office line is
excellent on a CV), transition value 4, trajectory 4, flexibility 3 → **3.6**
ODDS: profile match 2, referral **1**, speed 3 → **2.0**

**Verdict: apply only if she wants to be in Peak XV's system**, which is the real argument
for it. They place across a large portfolio and recruiters there see many roles; being a
known CV has option value beyond this one job. **As this specific role, she would be
building a distributor sales team from zero, which is the one thing on the requirement list
she has no evidence for.**
