# Rock Health Advisory — Consultant

Evaluated 2026-09-10.

## The frustrating shape of this one

**On paper it is the best-fitting role found in this entire search.** Scored as if she
could take it, it comes out at **FIT 4.6** — higher than Ferty9's 4.3, which has led the
board since August.

**Two independent blockers almost certainly rule it out anyway**, and either one alone is
fatal.

## Blocker one: work authorisation

The posting says *"primarily remote with office hubs in SF, NYC, and LA"* and states no
visa position. That silence is not an opening. Four things say this is a US-domestic role:

1. **All three hubs are US.**
2. **$95k–$105k is a US domestic band.** A firm hiring in India would post an India band.
3. **US employers saying "remote" nearly always mean remote within the US**, for payroll,
   tax and employment-law reasons. Employing someone in India requires an entity there or
   an employer-of-record arrangement.
4. **Rock Health Advisory is small.** Small US advisory firms rarely carry international
   employment infrastructure.

On sponsorship: Rock Health is a for-profit and therefore **not H-1B cap-exempt**, so the
same timing wall as the Paul Angell Foundation applies — a March lottery for an October
start, which does not fit a role being hired now.

## Blocker two: the timezone gate, which is hers

Even if authorisation were solved tomorrow, **Rock Health Advisory's clients are US
healthcare organisations** — payers, providers, digital health companies. Client interviews,
project calls and delivery all run on US hours.

**India timezone and no on-call is the one constraint Nikita has held absolutely since the
first day of this search.** A US advisory practice fails it structurally, not incidentally.

## Blocker three, quieter but real: the domain is the wrong country

Rock Health sells insight into the **US** healthcare market — its payer mix, its
reimbursement mechanics, its regulatory environment, its digital health funding cycles.

Nikita's expertise is **Indian** healthcare: government delivery systems, last-mile
constraints, philanthropic and public capital. That is genuinely valuable, and it is not
what this firm sells.

The one honest bridge is her in-progress paper **comparing the US and Indian health
economies**, which is real and relevant. It is a conversation starter, not four years of US
market experience.

---

## Verdict

**Do not apply. Do send one short enquiry.**

Applying costs an hour and will almost certainly be filtered on location. But the upside if
the answer is unexpectedly yes is the highest of anything on the board, and asking costs
five minutes. That asymmetry justifies the email and nothing more.

### The enquiry · 718 characters

> Hi, I'm interested in the Consultant role with Rock Health Advisory and wanted to check
> one thing before applying rather than waste your time.
>
> I'm based in Mumbai and I don't hold US work authorization. The posting says primarily
> remote with hubs in SF, NYC and LA, so I wanted to ask directly: is the role open to
> candidates outside the US, whether as an employee of record or a contractor, or is US
> authorization a requirement?
>
> If it's US only, no problem at all and thank you for reading. If it isn't, I'd love to
> apply. I've spent four years at Tata Trusts deciding which health technologies and
> delivery models were worth funding across a ₹450 crore portfolio, with health-tech and AI
> diagnostics as areas I own.

Naming the constraint first is the right move. It signals she reads carefully, it does not
waste their screening time, and the credential in the last line gives them a reason to
answer rather than ignore.

**If they say US only, close the row and move on.** Do not try to solve it — the H-1B
timing makes it unsolvable this year regardless.
