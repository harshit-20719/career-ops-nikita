# Scan — 17 August 2026

First scan run under the revised sector gate (`RUBRIC.md`, carve-out added today).

**Method changed, and this is the important part.** Previous scans produced *search URLs*
for Nikita to open by hand, because LinkedIn wouldn't enumerate from this environment.
That's now solved: LinkedIn's logged-out guest endpoints
(`/jobs-guest/jobs/api/seeMoreJobPostings/search` and `/jobs-guest/jobs/api/jobPosting/{id}`)
return live listings and full job descriptions to a plain browser User-Agent. No login,
no API key.

So this scan is real results rather than a to-do list: **153 postings pulled across 10
keyword sets, India geo-locked, posted in the last 14 days, mid-senior**. 92 survived a
title-relevance filter. Every job description quoted below was read in full from the
posting itself.

## What the gate change did

One role changed verdict, and it is a genuinely hard call rather than a formality.

### Evidence Action — Chief of Staff, AI Access Initiative (2AI India) · Delhi

Under the old gate this hard-failed on the `KNOWN_NONPROFIT` list without being read.
Under the carve-out it has to be read, so it was. The three tests:

| Test | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Healthcare is the domain, not one theme among many | **Fails.** 2AI spans health *and* agriculture "and other domains". Health is one of two launch programmes |
| Healthtech/AI is the substance of the job | **Partly.** The JD asks for "working familiarity with how AI and ML systems are built and deployed… enough to engage credibly with technical teams". But the day job is strategy design, coordination, government relations and grant reporting |
| Strong on its own terms | **Passes,** and comfortably |

On the letter of the rubric — fail one test, fail the gate — this is out. Two things
argue the other way and are worth putting in front of Nikita rather than deciding for her:

- The affiliations are unusual by any standard. Led by former Evidence Action CEO Kanika
  Bahl, a founding member of Anthropic's Long-Term Benefit Trust; advised by Michael
  Kremer, Dario Amodei and Kent Walker.
- 2AI India is explicitly "projected to spin off into an independent entity in the near
  term", and this role is "at the forefront of designing the strategy and execution plan".

Against that: **"grant reporting" is a listed responsibility**, and the surrounding
entity, EAII Advisors, does technical assistance to state governments on deworming and
supplementation. That is very close to the job Nikita is leaving. The bar is 5–6 years;
she is at 4y2m.

**Verdict: CHECK, not scored.** This is Nikita's call, not the rubric's. If the answer is
yes, it gets scored properly and the carve-out gets widened in writing to say so.

Nothing else on the board moved as a result of the gate change. The other non-profit hits
this week — Evidence Action's EVP Evidence role, and an ADB Investment Officer post in
New Delhi — are grant-making and development-finance respectively, neither centred on
healthtech or AI, and both stay out.

## Scan hygiene

`scripts/scan.py` still generates the search URLs, and its `screen()` function did the
gating for this run. Two things it can't see, because it only reads title, company and
location:

- **Offshore seats that don't say so in the title.** Novo Nordisk's GBS posting passed the
  screen and failed on reading. The `OFFSHORE_MARKERS` list already contains "gbs" — it
  just wasn't in any of the three fields the screen looks at.
- **Sector, now that the gate has a carve-out.** `KNOWN_NONPROFIT` still hard-fails by
  name, which is correct for grant-making but would have suppressed the Evidence Action
  role above without it ever being read. It is left as-is deliberately: a hard fail that
  gets manually overridden once a week is safer than a soft flag on a list that exists to
  catch the sector she is leaving.

The real fix for both is that full JDs are now fetchable, so the screen matters less than
it did — anything that survives on title can be read in full before it's scored.
