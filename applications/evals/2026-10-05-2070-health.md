## What the job actually is

Three blocks, and they are worth quoting rather than summarising:

**Whitespace identification** — market landscaping to "identify and size high-potential,
underserved healthcare segments in India through rigorous primary and secondary
research"; competitive and regulatory tracking; and opportunity validation through
"stakeholder and expert interviews, design and run rapid market pilots to pressure-test
hypotheses, assess problem severity, and establish conviction."

**Venture design** — translate validated whitespace into "investable venture concepts"
by defining business model, GTM and initial unit economics; **present the incubation
opportunity to IC for raising seed capital**; support the concept-to-launch sprint
alongside founders and EIRs.

**Value creation** — partner with founders on GTM, unit economics, pricing and growth;
advise on senior hiring and capital allocation; run the management reporting cadence
across portfolio companies, tracking KPIs, financials and operating metrics against plan.

Ventures"** — Mumbai, marked **Urgent**, and **posted a year ago**:

**So the first move is not the application form. It is one question to Jethwani** — which
resolves the staleness and converts a cold application into a referred one in the same
message. She has the channel open already.

## Risk flags

- **The alumni half of dimension 3 is unverified.** The studio is four years old and I
  could not trace New Ventures leavers. This is exactly the dimension the rubric's
  no-data rule exists to protect, so: the company signal is real and checkable (six
  ventures launched, 100% raised downstream capital, $30M backing), the alumni signal is
  not established. **Five minutes on LinkedIn — 2070 Health → People → past employees in
  New Ventures.** If that function churns, dimension 3 drops to 3 and FIT to 4.1.
- **The req has been open a year.** Either the bar is very specific or the seat keeps
  getting refilled from inside. Worth asking.
- **Venture studio economics.** The studio is funded by the fund. Her seat depends on
  Fund II deployment pace, and Fund II closed only four weeks ago, so the runway is as
  fresh as it gets — but it is one LP concentration away from a different answer.
- **Title optics.** "Associate" reads as a sideways step from Program Officer on paper.
  Inside venture it is the standard entry to the investing track, and the trajectory
  scores 5 for that reason. She should expect to explain the title to people outside
  the industry, and should not let that put her off.
- **Comp.** The form asks current and expected CTC. Numbers go in `private/`, not here.
