# Space tech — can she pitch into it?

Written 2026-09-07 at Nikita's request. She named Kepler Aero and Alteon, said she is not
asking to apply to those specifically, and acknowledged fit and odds would both be poor.

## Short answer

Yes, but not through the door she named.

**She asked about business development. In Indian space tech, BD is the worst possible
door for her**, and Digantara's current posting proves it precisely:

> *Director of Indian Govt Sales.* Requires a proven track record winning ₹10 Cr to ₹300 Cr+
> government and defence contracts, a strong existing network across the Armed Forces, DRDO,
> NTRO, the Ministries of Defence and Home, the NSA, ISRO and defence PSUs, **12 to 18+
> years**, and deep command of DAP/DPM procurement procedure.

In this sector BD means defence and government procurement. It is network gated in a way
no cover letter crosses. Fundraising has the same problem in reverse: space funds want
people who have closed hardware rounds.

**The door that is actually open is the founder's office**, which is the shape she has
already proven interest in and which asks for exactly the generalist skills she has.

## The live opening

**Bellatrix Aerospace, Founder's Office, Bengaluru** (posted 3 Sept, 200+ applicants).

Indian in-space propulsion company, over $32M raised, expanding into international markets
including the US. The role works directly with the founders across Strategy, Business
Development, Operations, Finance, Marketing, HR and Supply Chain.

The required skills, verbatim in substance: first-principles thinking and structured
problem solving, thriving in highly ambiguous environments, curiosity across business
functions, project management and execution, stakeholder management and cross-functional
collaboration, presenting recommendations to senior leadership, and a process improvement
mindset focused on building scalable frameworks.

**Not one of those is aerospace specific.** Every one of them describes what she does.

**The gap, stated plainly:** they ask for **6 to 10 years** and she is at 4 years 2 months.
The listed backgrounds are consulting, startups, venture capital, technology and product
management, and hers is none of those. She has no space domain at all.

That is a real two year shortfall against a selective role with 200+ applicants. It is
also the only space tech opening found in a month of scanning that she could credibly hold.

## What a real pitch would rest on

Three claims, all backed by things already in her materials. None of them require inventing
space experience.

1. **Government systems.** She structured programmes to work *through* existing government
   systems and schemes rather than duplicating them, and built those linkages deliberately.
   India's space sector runs on ISRO, IN-SPACe and defence procurement. Someone who
   understands how to move inside government machinery rather than around it is genuinely
   useful, even without a defence network.
2. **Evaluating hard technology without being an engineer.** Health-tech and AI diagnostics
   are sub-themes she owned. She assessed technologies on technical maturity, deployment
   readiness and evidence quality, and structured pilots under a deliberately heavier
   evidence bar. That is the transferable claim: she can hold a technical conversation and
   form a commercial judgement from it.
3. **Partnerships from cold.** 15+ institutional partnerships originated and closed, from
   finding the target through to structuring terms.

**What she must not do is claim space knowledge.** In a sector this technical, a
non-engineer who overclaims is discovered in the first conversation. The honest position is
stronger: she brings the commercial and government layer, and will learn the domain.

## Honest scoring

Bellatrix would land around **FIT 3.4 / ODDS 2.0** if scored properly. The fit is decent
because a founder's office is function agnostic; the odds are poor because of the two year
gap and 200+ applicants. That is roughly IQVIA's shape.

**This is a lottery ticket she wants to buy, and it costs an hour.** That is a legitimate
use of an hour when the alternative is a ninth kit for a role she is ambivalent about.
It should not displace the Bridgespan email or the HealthKois note.
