## The one angle that would change the odds

**TAG has a dedicated AI practice** — the JD asks for "research and thought leadership
products for **TAG AI**."

Health AI policy in India is a real, narrow, and commercially valuable niche that a
tech-policy team staffed from fintech and digital-platform backgrounds may well not cover.
She has: four years funding and evaluating AI diagnostics, hands-on judgement about what
survives deployment in Indian conditions, the government-systems instinct, and an
in-progress paper on the US versus Indian health economies.

**If she applies, that is the application** — not "I am interested in tech policy," but "your
AI practice has a health gap and here is the specific thing I know about it." It also gives
the cover letter something to be about, which matters when the portal is the only door.

## Verdict

**Don't prioritise it. Apply only if the cover letter can be built around the health-AI
angle above**, and treat it as a slow-burn, low-probability application rather than one of
the week's moves.

Three practical notes if she does:

1. **It needs a cover letter, resume *and* references.** References is the same blocker as
   Health Progress Hub — she needs two people lined up, and partner-organisation contacts
   remain the right answer.
2. **Ask about hours before any later-stage conversation.** A DC-headquartered firm serving
   global clients, with an unstated working window, is exactly the shape that has caught her
   before.
3. **Five days in-person in New Delhi.** Delhi NCR is her P1 tier, not P0, and this is a
   full relocation with no stated flexibility. Worth being certain about before writing a
   cover letter, not after an offer.

## What this role is genuinely useful for knowing

**It is the clearest commercial policy-advisory role she has seen**, and the lane is real:
corporate public policy and government affairs in India pays well, and her mix of health
domain, government-systems experience and published research is a better fit for that lane
than for most of the strategy roles she has been chasing.

If TAG itself does not work, **that lane is worth a deliberate look** — big-tech and
pharma public policy and government-affairs roles in India, where the health-plus-policy
combination is the qualification rather than a curiosity. That is a more useful takeaway
than this one application.
