# Three-lane scan — results

Run 2026-09-07. **673 postings pulled**, covering keyword searches plus every named target
firm in all three lanes. Nine job descriptions read.

---

## The headline is uncomfortable

**Broadening into these lanes produced fewer applicable postings than the healthcare
strategy lane did, not more — and the ones that qualify sit closer to the job she is
leaving rather than further from it.**

| Lane | Postings at named target firms | Applicable to her |
| --- | --- | --- |
| L1 — impact investing / DFI | 6, mostly wrong level | **1** |
| L2 — healthcare VC / PE | **0** | 0 |
| L3 — impact consulting | ~13, concentrated in one firm | 2 |

The lanes are not a supply fix. They are a *fit* fix for a small number of specific
targets, and two of the three have to be worked through outreach rather than applications.

### Lightroad — Manager, Strategic Partnerships · **FIT 3.3 / ODDS 3.3**

Blended finance platform bringing philanthropic, public and commercial capital together
across climate, livelihoods, financial inclusion, **healthcare**, education and skilling.
That intersection is genuinely the one she has described wanting.

She clears the stated bar cleanly, which is rare:

- **2–5 years** in *"fundraising, strategic partnerships, philanthropy, CSR, impact
  investing, development finance"* — **philanthropy is named**
- Degree in *"Business, Management, Economics…"* — BA Economics (Hons) qualifies
- *"Strong network across CSR, philanthropy foundations, family offices, impact investors,
  DFIs"* — four years at Tata Trusts is exactly this, and it is not a claim she has to
  stretch

**But read the mandate before getting excited.** The responsibilities are developing a
pipeline of funders, managing fundraising and capital mobilisation, writing grant
proposals, concept notes and RFP responses, and managing funder relationships after money
is secured.

**This is a fundraising role, not an investment role.** It is the mirror image of her job —
she would be raising capital for Lightroad's facilities rather than deploying it. Her
genuine edge is that she knows how funders decide because she has been one, which makes
her good at this. The cost is that two years in it makes her a fundraiser, not an investor.

That is why transition value scores 3 and the overall lands at 3.3 rather than 4-plus. If
the objective is the buy side, this is sideways.

## Lane 2 — healthcare VC and PE

**Zero postings. Not one, across HealthQuad, W Health Ventures, Somerset Indus, Quadria,
InvAscent, Chiratae and Sabre Partners.**

This is now confirmed rather than suspected. HealthQuad's only investment-team role is
framed as a pre-MBA seat at 2–3 years, below her band.

**Lane 2 is an outreach lane. It cannot be worked through applications, and no amount of
scanning will change that.** It is also, on fit, the best of the three — healthcare domain
plus organisational diligence is a combination almost nobody brings to a healthcare fund.

The correct move is the Cent and Kae Capital pattern: a researched brief and a direct note
to a partner, where there is no screen to fail. HealthQuad first (~$280M AUM, Delhi,
India's leading healthcare-focused fund), then W Health and Somerset Indus in Mumbai.

## Lane 3 — social impact consulting

The only lane with a real pipeline, and it is concentrated in one firm.

**Athena Infonomics (Delhi) has roughly ten open roles**, most under a new entity called
APLYD doing AI in public systems for governments, multilaterals and DFIs. The most
relevant, *Manager, AI and Digital Transformation (Rural Economy)*, asks for four-plus
years in **rural development, financial inclusion, digital products or public-sector
programme delivery**, and requires that *"you have specified or managed a digital product
or module that went into use, and you can explain what you got wrong the first time."*
She has not. The domain is rural livelihoods, not health. **Not a fit, despite the AI
framing.**

**Sattva Consulting — Senior Consultant, CSR Advisory, Mumbai.** P0, no relocation. CSR
strategy, programme design, funder engagement, client relationship ownership. She would be
strong at it immediately — and that is the problem. Advising corporates on how to deploy
CSR money is adjacent to deploying philanthropic money, which is the job she is leaving.
Worth applying to only as a floor, not as a goal.

**Bridgespan — still no posting**, consistent with the 2026 cycle being closed. It remains
the best credential match found anywhere in this search, and it needs one direct email
rather than more scanning.

---

## What this changes

1. **Stop scanning Lane 2.** It has no postings and will not have any. Convert it to
   outreach — a HealthQuad brief on the Cent/Kae pattern is the single highest-value thing
   in these three lanes.
2. **Lane 1 is a network lane too.** One applicable posting out of an entire month across
   ten named funds. The funds are real targets; the job boards are not the route to them.
3. **Lane 3 has supply but poor transition value.** Bridgespan is the exception and it is
   unposted. Sattva is a floor option.
4. **The three best things available remain unposted or unsent**: the Bridgespan email,
   the HealthQuad brief, and the six kits already written and still not sent.

The honest summary is that widening the search has clarified *where to point outreach*,
not produced a queue of applications.
