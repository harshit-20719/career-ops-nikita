f_TPR=r604800 limits to the past week; f_E=4 filters to mid-senior. Swap the keywords for "Corporate Strategy", "Investment Associate", or "Business Strategy" to widen. And run the same searches on iimjobs — it's the strongest board for your profile and it's the one I couldn't reach.
