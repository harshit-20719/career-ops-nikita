04

They will ask

"Rate your financial modelling, 1 to 5."

The form asks this outright, so decide the number before you open it. Do not say 4. Say 3 and define it in the same breath: budgets, utilisation, variance and cost-per-beneficiary across a $51M portfolio, plus market sizing and unit economics from Tiff.in. Then name the gap yourself: you have not built a valuation or a cap table. A defined 3 is more credible than an undefended 4, and this is a firm whose whole model is evidence bars.

"How many years in consulting?" with only 1 to 4 on offer

There is no zero and the field is required. You do have advisory work that is honestly consulting-shaped: you advised partners on mobilising co-funding and government funding alongside Trust capital, and you wrote policy briefs for senior management. Selecting the lowest option and then correcting it in the first lines of the cover note is defensible. This is your call, not mine, and it is the strongest single argument for going through Jethwani, where the form does not exist.

"Why leave a ₹450 crore portfolio for an Associate seat?"

Answer the title question head on rather than hoping it does not come up. The portfolio is scale without a feedback loop: you recommend, capital goes out, and the signal that comes back is a report rather than a result. Their model has an IC, a launch and a P&L. That is the thing you are buying, and Associate is what the entry to it is called.

"Walk us through a whitespace you would back in Indian healthcare."

Near-certain, and it is the real test. Prepare one, with a size, a reason nobody has taken it, and how you would kill it cheaply in eight weeks. Build it out of a sub-theme you owned rather than something read in a report, and bring the pilot design, because their stated method is exactly that.

"Which of our ventures would you have argued against?"

A studio that spends 12 to 18 months validating will respect a reasoned no far more than enthusiasm. Pick one of the six, form a real view, and say what evidence would change your mind.
