## The framing point that shaped the CV

**Fireside Ventures is a consumer-brand fund, not a healthcare fund.** In their own
words, they are "focused exclusively on building iconic and purposeful consumer brands."
₹5,300 crore managed, **66+ brands**, 33 people, Bengaluru. Fund IV closed at ₹2,265
crore. Portfolio revenue around $1.6B and market value above $7B. Mamaearth (Honasa),
boAt and Yoga Bar are theirs.

So even Fitterfly and Amaha are backed here as **consumer health brands.** Whoever reads
this CV is a consumer operator, not a hospital administrator.

**That inverts what has to lead.** On every other version this session, her healthcare
depth was the qualification. Here **the healthcare depth is the differentiator and the
consumer-brand record is the qualification** — and the CV is swapped accordingly:
partnership lifecycle rather than grant lifecycle, origination targets rather than
grantmaking targets, the sub-themes named explicitly so a fertility or mental-health
founder sees their own category, and the Tiff.in growth bullet widened to say she owned
content, publishing and distribution end to end.

One useful detail: Fireside markets purpose — "doing well by doing good," with a
Responsible Investment Policy on their own footer. **Her development-sector background
reads as an asset in this portfolio rather than something to explain away.** That is not
true of most of her board.

### What changed in the CV, and what did not

Word swaps only, per her standing rule — structure, writing style and content are
unchanged, and the climate-health bullet stays off. One trim was needed: the 2020 economic
slowdown paper lost a bullet, because the first build spilled to two pages at 694 words.

## What to ask the person circulating it

Three things, and the first one is the only one that really matters.

1. **"Please tell me which companies you actually sent it to."** A blind circulation she
   cannot see is a channel with no follow-up. Knowing the five or six names turns it into
   five or six warm leads she can chase herself in a fortnight.
2. **"Can you add the blurb above when you forward it?"** The CV alone does not say what
   she wants to be considered for, and nobody reading it will guess founder's office.
3. **"Which of these are actually hiring on the business side right now?"** Someone inside
   the Fireside network knows which portfolio companies have an open seat, and that is
   information she cannot get from outside. It is also the cheapest question in this list.
