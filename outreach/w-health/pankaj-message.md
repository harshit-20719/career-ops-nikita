## Why this is a better fit than any fund found so far

**Their model is not classic venture.** The in-house team identifies a gap in healthcare
delivery, spends **12 to 18 months evaluating and de-risking the opportunity**, and only
then recruits a founding team to build a company around it.

That matters enormously for Nikita, because the thing that has killed every mainstream VC
application is deal execution. Artha was explicit: *"must have closed at least 2 investments
end-to-end on the buy-side"* and *"candidates with only investment banking, transaction
advisory, or consulting backgrounds will not be considered."*

**W Health's core work is not deal execution. It is problem identification and validation
before a company exists.** That is what she does:

| Their model | Her job |
| --- | --- |
| Identify gaps in healthcare delivery | 40+ programmes across health-tech, AI diagnostics, maternal, immunisation, geriatric, tribal, primary and tertiary care |
| Spend 12–18 months evaluating and de-risking | Two-to-three-month design cycles, technology pilots structured under a deliberately heavier evidence bar |
| Decide whether the opportunity is real before committing capital | 30–50 proposals a month screened, deep-dive diligence, recommendation to CEO and Board |
| Choose where and how to test | A geospatial analysis showing identical interventions producing materially different returns by geography |

And a fund with fifty physicians, operators and technologists is a fund that hires
non-investment specialists. That is a structurally different door from a five-person seed
fund where every hire must have closed deals.
