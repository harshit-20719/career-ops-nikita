# Cent — company brief and outreach angle

Researched 2026-08-13. Everything below is sourced from published reporting or Cent's
own site; where sources conflict or I could not verify, it says so.

## What the company is

**Cent** (legal/email domain `centai.in`, site `cent.health`) is a Bengaluru preventive-
healthcare company. It sells one thing: a single fixed full-body screening protocol for
asymptomatic people, called **CCNM** — Cardiac, Cancer, Neurological, Metabolic.

- Full-body MRI (multi-sequence, ~30 min), CT calcium scoring, DEXA, ECG, echo,
  mammogram for women, 100+ blood and urine biomarkers, optional genetics module
- One 2-hour visit, AI analysis, mandatory doctor consultation within 48 hours
- **₹27,500** for the protocol (site shows a ₹22,500–45,000 band); genetics +₹25,000
- No packages, no tiers. If you skip a component, they won't issue a report at all

**Founded** January 2025; commercially live since the start of FY26.

### Money and partners

- **$5M seed**, announced Jan 2026 — **OneFlow Holdings** (Shashank's family office) and
  **South Park Commons**
- **Siemens Healthineers** partnership: powers imaging across the centre network, custom
  MRI protocols developed jointly, plus stated joint research on preventive imaging
  standards

### Footprint and the plan

- **One centre today** — 7,000 sq ft flagship in Bengaluru, opened April 2026
- **Plan: 20–25 centres in 6–9 months** across Bengaluru, Delhi, Mumbai, Hyderabad,
  Pune, Chennai, Kolkata — then 15 more cities
- Capex is their largest cost line

Competitors named in coverage: Pristyn Care, Thyrocare, Superhealth domestically;
Prenuvo and Function Health globally.

## Where she actually fits

Three live problems, in the order they're worth leading with.

### 1. Where to open next — the strongest angle

They are about to spend most of a $5M seed putting 20–25 physical centres into seven
cities in nine months. Sequencing that wrong is the most expensive mistake available to
them, and it is a catchment and geography question, not a real-estate question.

Nikita built **a geospatial heat map of every rupee Tata Trusts deployed across 26
Indian states, measuring return by geography and sub-theme** — showing identical
interventions producing materially different returns by region — and it shaped portfolio
allocation from 2022 to 2024.

That is the same artifact Cent needs, built once already, on a ₹450 crore portfolio.
Nobody they'd otherwise hire for expansion has done this.

`[VERIFY]` "26 states" is still unconfirmed in the master resume. Confirm before sending.

### 2. The corporate line they haven't built

The site has a **Corporate** nav item and an "Employee Wellness" mention behind it — no
pricing, no named clients, no described programme. At ₹27,500 a head with a 45-year-old
male-skewed D2C customer, employer channels are the obvious next unit of distribution,
and it's undeveloped.

Nikita **originated and closed 15+ institutional partnerships** — sourcing the target,
building the relationship, structuring the terms. That's the job.

### 3. The evidence gap

Cent's science page claims an 83% "Early Detection Index" against 15–20% for a
traditional checkup. It names **no clinical advisory board, no published validation, no
named researchers**, and says nothing about false positives, incidental findings, or
overdiagnosis.

That's their sharpest exposure. Screening asymptomatic people with whole-body MRI is
genuinely contested — the standard critique is incidentalomas driving cascade testing —
and it's the first thing a Series A diligence process or a serious journalist will press.

Nikita **structured technology pilots under a heavier evidence bar** at the Trusts:
evaluating tech across varied conditions and selecting geographies that limited community
risk and confounders. That is validation-study design. Her partner institutions include
**IISc, C-CAMP and NIMHANS — all Bengaluru** — plus the George Institute for Global
Health and Apollo.

**Do not raise this in a first email.** It reads as an attack from a stranger. It is an
excellent thing to bring to a second conversation, once they want something from her.

### And the hook that makes the email land

She is already writing a paper **comparing the US and Indian health economies, arguing
for increased funding in preventive healthcare**. That is Cent's entire thesis, and she
was writing it before they were on her radar. It is true, it is unusual, and it is the
one sentence that separates her note from every other cold email in the founder's inbox.
