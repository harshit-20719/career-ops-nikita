# HealthKois — brief and outreach angle

Researched 2026-09-07.

## Why this, and not HealthQuad

HealthQuad was the obvious target — India's leading healthcare VC, ~$280M AUM. But its
leadership split. **Charles Janssen, Ajay Mahipal and Dr. Pinak Shrikhande left and
launched HealthKois**, a successor fund, while HealthQuad raises its own $300M under the
remaining IC (Abrar Mir, Dr. Amit Varma, Sunil Thakur, Neeraj Chandra, Rahul Agarwal).

**A fund that has just spun out is a far better cold-outreach target than an established
one.** It is building a team, has no formal hiring process, and every conversation is with
someone who can decide.

## Write to Ajay Mahipal

India-based partner on the investment side. Before HealthQuad: Currae Healthtech Fund,
Indian Angel Network, Elara Capital, and Senior VP Investment Banking at Srei. He is the
working-level India investor, which is the right altitude — Charles Janssen as Managing
Partner will be consumed by the raise.

## The angle

A fund building 13–16 positions from zero over four years needs diligence capacity more
than it needs anything else. Nikita has assessed 40+ organisations and their leadership on
capability, governance and capacity to deploy capital, screened 30–50 proposals a month
against a fixed framework, and evaluated health-tech and AI diagnostics specifically on
technical maturity, deployment readiness and evidence quality.

Four of their five focus areas — healthtech, life sciences, medtech, healthcare delivery —
are areas she has evaluated with real money behind the judgement.

### The fifth one, and it is her call

**Climate Health is one of the five stated focus areas.** At the Trusts, Nikita redirected
mainstream health funding toward multi-thematic projects, particularly climate and health,
where no allocation previously existed.

Very few candidates in India can say that, and it maps directly onto one of their five
focus areas.

She pulled that bullet from her CV on 11 August and confirmed on 17 August that it stays
off every version. **This brief does not put it in the message.** It is flagged here
because the opportunity is unusually specific and the decision is hers — using it in a
conversation is a different choice from putting it on a resume, and the master resume
already notes it stays available for interviews.

## Before sending

1. Send Tuesday–Thursday morning IST.
2. Do not send to both funds in the same week. They split recently; the ecosystem is small
   and people compare notes.
3. Decide the climate-health question before the call, not during it.
4. A fund mid-raise moves slowly. The realistic outcome is a relationship now and a seat
   when the fund closes — which is how venture hiring works anyway.
