# Eka Care — company brief, role audit and outreach angle

Researched 2026-10-05. Read all 24 live openings from their Keka board.

**The headline is an awkward one: this is the best-matched *company* I have looked at
against her actual thesis, and it has no role for her.** One posted role is a real but
unexciting option at **FIT 3.3 / ODDS 3.0**, and the genuinely interesting move here is
outreach about a seat that does not exist yet.

### Why this company fits her thesis better than anything else on the board

Her single most distinctive design principle, straight from the master resume:

> **Built government linkages deliberately to avoid creating parallel infrastructure** —
> structuring programmes to work through existing government systems and schemes rather
> than duplicating them.

**ABDM Connect is that principle sold as a product.** Their own description: it "helps
hospitals, labs, clinics, and HIS/EMR vendors go live on India's health stack — ABHA
verification, HIP/HIU flows, consent-based health record exchange — without building or
certifying it themselves."

That is an unusually exact match, and it is checkable rather than asserted. Add that
**health-tech and AI diagnostics were her owned sub-themes**, so she has spent four years
forming views on which health technologies were ready to deploy and which worked once in
one place — and Eka Care's entire bet is clinical AI running in real consultations at
national scale.

### The risk that has to sit next to all of that

- **FY24: ₹34.8 crore revenue against a ₹13.8 crore loss.** Not profitable.
- **The Series A was 2022. No Series B has been disclosed in four years**, and total
  disclosed funding is $19.5M. For a loss-making company that is the main thing to
  understand before joining.
- `[VERIFY]` **Headcount data conflicts between third-party trackers.** One reports 182
  employees and a 16% year-on-year *fall*; another reports 132 as of August and a 21%
  *rise*. Both are scrapers, neither is reliable, and the direction matters. She should
  ask directly.
- Against that: they have **24 roles open, ten of them engineering and ML**, which is not
  what a company in retreat does. Either a round is close or the AI products are pulling
  real revenue. **That is the single best question to ask.**

## The role audit — all 24 openings

Everything is **Bengaluru, on-site**. The distribution tells the story: **6 data science
and ML, 4 engineering, 4 product, 2 customer success, 2 marketing, 1 sales, 1 inside
sales, 1 medical affairs, and 3 interns.**

**There is no strategy role, no chief of staff, no founder's office, no corporate
development, and no partnerships lead.** This is a deep-tech AI build-out, and the
business side is being staffed with sales and product rather than strategy.

### Dead on arrival

**Senior Medical Officer – Clinical AI** — **"MBBS + MD (Internal Medicine)"**, 3–5 years
post-MD. A hard degree gate, and it would be the twentieth. Also a 5-month contract on a
six-day week.

Painful, because the *work* is hers almost line for line: "building a clinical evaluation
framework to assess how well AI systems handle real-world medical practice in India…
build scoring rubrics and contribute to evaluation methodology." That is her heavier
evidence bar, in product form. **She cannot have it, because she is not a physician.**

### Profile fail, not a gate fail

**Product Manager - AI** (2–6 yrs) and **Product Manager – EkaAgents** (3–5 yrs).

Three of her bullets would be genuinely strong — "define what good looks like for your
product's output, and build the evaluation systems that hold it to that bar", "translate
ambiguous clinical problems into scoped product behaviors with explicit guardrails", and
"lead enterprise and government client engagements."

But the must-haves are not negotiable and she holds none of them:

- "**2–6 years of product management experience**" — she has none
- "**Hands-on experience building AI products** such as voice AI, conversational AI, or agents"
- "Genuine fluency with large language models and agentic systems, able to reason about
  prompts, tool use, evaluations, and failure modes **without needing a translator**"
- "Technical understanding of how APIs and SDKs work, **backed by hands-on experience
  shipping against them**"

EkaAgents is harder still — it wants someone who builds "LLM-as-judge frameworks" and
seeds adversarial test cases. **Her AI exposure is as a funder evaluating AI, not as
someone who has shipped it.** That "without needing a translator" clause is there
specifically to screen out her profile, and no cover letter gets round it.

**Customer Success Manager** asks for 1–3 years in client onboarding and training. She is
*above* the band and the scope is doctor onboarding. Not worth an hour.

pipeline is thin** — and right now it is not: 2070 Health is at 4.3/4.0 and unsent.

## The actual move: write to Vikalp Sahni

A company with 24 openings, ten of them engineering and ML, selling into hospitals, labs,
insurers and government — and **not one strategy, partnerships or business-development
hire.** That is either deliberate or a gap. Asking which is a reasonable question, and it
is the kind of question a founder answers.

**Vikalp Sahni, CEO.** Ex-Goibibo, so he has built and sold a company before and will
read a short, specific note. `linkedin.com/company/ekacare` lists the company; his profile
is findable from the team page.

### Drafts — all under 200 characters, no dashes. Send A.

**A — the parallel-infrastructure line · 176 characters**

> Hi Vikalp, four years funding Indian health programmes taught me one rule: never build
> parallel infrastructure. ABDM Connect is that idea, commercialised. Worth a conversation?

This is the one to send. It is a real principle she actually held, it maps onto their
flagship product without flattering them, and it signals domain fluency in one line.

**B — the evaluation angle · 180 characters**

> Hi Vikalp, I spent four years deciding which Indian health technologies were ready to
> deploy and which worked once. You're now doing that on clinical AI at scale. Worth 15
> minutes?

**C — names the gap directly · 193 characters**

> Hi Vikalp, you have 24 roles open and ten are engineering. Nothing on the government and
> enterprise side. I've built health partnerships through government systems for four
> years. Worth a chat?

Higher risk, higher reward. It proves she read the careers page rather than the funding
announcement. **Only send this if she is comfortable being told the gap is intentional** —
some founders will read it as presumptuous, and an ex-Goibibo CEO may well have a reason.

**D — follow-up, only after ~10 days · 173 characters**

> Hi Vikalp, following up. Happy to send one page on where I think ABDM Connect loses deals
> on the government side, no obligation. Four years of health partnerships behind it.

As with Primerry: **if she is willing to write that page, offer it in the first message
rather than the fourth.** A work sample gets read when a CV does not.

## Before she writes

1. **Lead with ABDM, not the portfolio.** The ₹450 crore number is not the point here. The
   parallel-infrastructure principle is, because it is the thing their flagship product
   sells.
2. **Install the Eka Care app and create an ABHA through it**, or at least open EkaScribe's
   product page. It takes ten minutes and it is the difference between having read about
   them and having used them.
3. **Ask about the funding gap, but not in the first message.** Four years since the
   Series A, with losses, while hiring ten engineers is the central fact about this
   company. It is a fair question on a call and a rude one in a cold note.
4. **Do not pitch herself for the Product Manager roles.** She fails three stated
   must-haves and the "without needing a translator" line is explicitly screening her
   profile out. Applying would spend credibility she needs for the conversation that
   matters.
5. **Decide in advance how she feels about a sales track**, in case the Technical Sales
   Manager role is what they offer. It is genuinely commercial healthcare work and it
   would break the philanthropy framing. It is also not the track she has been aiming at,
   and that is worth having thought about before someone asks.
6. **Investor-side route, if the direct note goes nowhere:** 3one4 Capital and Verlinvest.
   Verlinvest also backs Ferty9, where she already has an application in. Thin, but it is
   a thread, and it is the only one here.

## What to go in knowing

- **The best-matched company of the search, with no matching role.** That gap is the whole
  reason this is an outreach brief rather than an application kit.
- **The most relevant posted role requires an MD.** Twentieth credential wall, and the
  closest one yet to work she has actually done.
- **The second-most relevant requires shipped-product experience** she does not have.
- **Four years without a disclosed round**, loss-making, conflicting headcount reports.
  Real risk, and it belongs in a conversation rather than in a decision made from outside.
