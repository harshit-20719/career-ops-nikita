# Intake questionnaire

Fill this in once. Everything downstream — resume versions, tailored applications,
outreach drafts, comp strategy — reads from it, so it's worth an unhurried hour.

Answer in whatever depth you have. `?` or `unknown` is a fine answer and is far better
than a guess: anything written here gets treated as fact later on.

> **Where to keep the filled copy.** If this repo is public, put it at
> `private/intake.md` (gitignored) or make the repo private first. Compensation floors
> and phone numbers do not belong in public git history, and history persists even after
> a later deletion.
