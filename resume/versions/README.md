## Three things to fix before either goes out

### 1. Fund figure — RESOLVED, now fix both PDFs

**₹450 crore / $51M portfolio under management.** Confirmed 2026-08-11. The "$15M" on
the healthcare version was a typo for $51M.

Correct both PDFs to this wording:

> Managed a **₹450 crore ($51M)** healthcare grant portfolio across 40+ programmes

And **delete the per-cycle deployment figure** from the generalist version ("$41M in
capital per cycle"). Portfolio-under-management is the bigger, cleaner, fully confirmed
number; the annual deployment figure is still ambiguous and adds nothing the $51M
doesn't already say.

### 2. "Internal Use Only" is stamped on the generalist PDF

The header of `Nikita_Sachanandani__g.pdf` carries an **"Internal Use Only"** marker —
almost certainly from a Tata Trusts document template. Remove it before sending. It
looks careless at best, and at worst suggests the resume was written on employer
document infrastructure.

### 3. Missing material that belongs on both

From the intake narrative, absent from both PDFs — each of these is stronger than
something currently on the page:

- **The dual Program Officer + Grants Manager role**, the only person on the team with
  two mandates
- **Founder and programme-leader due diligence**, explicitly modelled on pre-investment
  assessment — the most transferable thing you do for a VC audience
- **Financial gatekeeper for the entire health theme**, not just own programmes —
  budget review across every partner before CEO and Board approval
- **Created the climate-health vertical from zero** — designed the thesis, secured the
  funding, stood it up where nothing existed. "Built a new investment vertical from
  scratch" is a founder's-office headline
- **Government-linkage design constraint** — structuring programmes through existing
  state systems specifically to avoid parallel infrastructure
- **Earlier six-sub-theme, 40-parameter grant evaluation and Board advisory scope**

Currently on the healthcare version. It reads as credible access to some interviewers

## Naming going forward

`YYYY-MM-<role-slug>.md` — e.g. `2026-08-healthcare.md`, `2026-08-generalist.md`. Record
the stem in the `resume_version` column of `applications.csv` so you always know what a
given company read.

## A third version worth building

For **MBB / consulting** applications specifically. Consulting screens read for
structured problem-solving, quantitative rigour, and top-tier academics — so lead with
the Young India Fellowship and the economics honours, foreground the heat map as an
analytical artefact, and cut the clinical sub-theme detail that matters to a health-tech
reader but not to a generalist consulting screen.
