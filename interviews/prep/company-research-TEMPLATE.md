## Culture signals

Engineering blog, open-source repos, conference talks, public writing from the team.
Reviews on Glassdoor and similar, read skeptically — people mostly write reviews when
angry, so weight the specifics, not the ratings.

## My questions for them

Specific, and answerable only by someone who works there. Not "what's the culture like".

1. What does the first 90 days look like for this role?
2. What's the hardest problem the team is working on right now?
3. How does this team decide what to build?
4.
5.

Keep two in reserve for the final round — they'll ask again and repeating yourself is a
missed opportunity.

## Red flags to watch for

- Role scope shifting between conversations
- Nobody can describe what success looks like at six months
- Evasion about why the seat is open
- Recent unexplained departures at your level
